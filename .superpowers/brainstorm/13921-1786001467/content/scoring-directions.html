B · Layered decision scorecard

Recommended. Separates measurement health, outcome, process, reliability, and specification coverage. Produces a merge answer without erasing nuance.

C · Evidence-ledger first

Scientifically honest and excellent for triage, but too dense as the primary Actions summary. Better as the drill-down beneath B.

Recommendation

Use B as the reading order, C as its evidence layer

Do not publish a universal composite score. A run first proves it produced valid measurements, then reports independent performance dimensions, then links every aggregate to cells and evidence.
