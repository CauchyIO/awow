# Output discipline

Non-negotiable.

## Rule 1 — Minimum useful body

A story body is the smallest set of sentences that lets a competent teammate pick up
the work: one-sentence intent, then acceptance criteria. No meeting recap, no
"Context" section, no narrative of who said what.

## Rule 2 — Placement

| Kind of content | Goes in |
|---|---|
| Intent + acceptance criteria | Issue body |
| Status, blockers, decisions during execution | Issue `## Comments` entry |
| Durable rationale ("how we do X") | `context/knowledge-base/` |

## Rule 3 — Update vs. edit

Body edits are reserved for scope or acceptance-criteria changes; everything else is
a comment.
